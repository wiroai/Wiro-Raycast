# Wiro AI Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Generate Image: pick a Wiro AI image model, write a prompt, set its options with the price shown for your settings, then copy, save or open the image
- Generate Video: make a video from a prompt or from an image on your Mac, follow the task status, cancel it, and open, copy or save the video
- Ask AI: chat with LLMs through the Wiro AI LLM gateway, choose the model, follow up in the same conversation, and copy or paste answers
- Search Models: search the Wiro AI catalog by name or category, see details and prices, open model pages or start a generation with a model
